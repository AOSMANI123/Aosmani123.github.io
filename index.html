Method

How I work a problem

The same four steps, whether the product is an AI system, a research study, or a meeting nobody wanted to sit through.

  1. Step 1

    Interview the users

    25 structured discovery interviews in my first six weeks at Amazon, across product, engineering, internal comms and PR.

  2. Step 2

    Find the real constraint

    The interviews surfaced it: the platform exposed no write APIs. Everything after that was designed around it.

  3. Step 3

    Ship

    A working seven-agent system within eight weeks of joining, handling a 200+ item portfolio.

  4. Step 4

    Measure whether it moved

    Coverage, volume and false positives for monitoring. Update time for tracking, which fell 80% at Microsoft.
